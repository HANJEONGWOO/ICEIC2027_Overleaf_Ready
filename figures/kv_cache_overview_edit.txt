Visual design (2026-11-06, simplified technical diagram):
  Plain (a)/(b)/(c) titles replace the numbered tiles. No card headers,
  accent bars, shadows, gradients, or stacked decorative tensor slices.
  Thin outlines, straight connectors, two flat matrix grids and direct text
  labels provide a conventional PowerPoint/block-diagram appearance.
  The three original stages remain: asymmetric KV index budget; Boundary
  allocation; hardware-aware KV codecs. The K4/V2 Lloyd--Max codecs stay fixed;
  PU-K8 targets arithmetic indexing and HVX decoding in the K8 layers.
  The drawing depicts a fixed mapping, not an optimal or adaptive policy.
  Key/value index-storage bar is exactly 3:1 (average K6 / V2, excluding FP16
  scales). Its eight-bit total is explicitly labeled as an average.
  Qwen3-1.7B layers 1--7 and 22--28 use K8; 8--21 use K4; V2 throughout.
  K outputs connect to their selected codec titles; both K8 branches merge
  into PU-K8. The white gap at the one crossing means no connection, and only
  the true K8 merge has a filled dot. Pale K8 fills distinguish those groups.
  Quantizer plots use white backgrounds and the same standardized x axis.
  PU-K8 uses 32 short neutral ticks sampled symmetrically from its 256 levels;
  K4/V2 show all their levels as dots. The center fine region
  is blue, both coarse tails use the same muted orange, and V2 uses green.
  Gray dashed sample traces are drawn over the Gaussian model so that their
  close agreement does not make the sampled evidence disappear.
  The two-column figure dimensions are unchanged to preserve paper layout.
  This is an AI-assisted, code-generated editable diagram. The visual style
  does not claim manual authorship or remove the need to disclose actual AI
  assistance under applicable publication rules.

Data:
  kv_cache_overview_levels.json stores all 16/256/4 actual FP16 decoder levels,
  original source paths and hashes, the PU-K8 parameters and exact midpoint.
  PU-K8 displays one of every eight levels in each positive magnitude region
  (offset 4), reflected to the negative side: 32 actual level positions total.
  The codec retains 256 levels; this subset is documented here and in the
  plotting source. The paper caption omits the detailed distribution notes.
  K4/V2 show all 16/4 actual levels as dots. The per-head dimension footer
  is omitted from the overview. No decoder levels or density data are altered.
  Smooth curves: the design model N(0,1/128) in y, equivalently N(0,1) in
  z=sqrt(128)*y. This model is not fitted to the plotted samples.
  Gray traces: densities from captured Qwen3-1.7B W4A16 HTP FP16-KV outputs,
  transformed offline with the existing CPU FP16 normalization/rotation
  emulator. They are not directly captured post-rotation HTP coordinates.
  Two validation documents, 28 layers, 8 KV heads, 32 valid sampled token
  positions per document/layer; total 1,835,008 scalar coordinates per K/V.
  K4 plot: middle layers; K8 plot: boundary layers; V2 plot: all layers.
  Source hashes, valid-token checks and bins are retained in
  kv_cache_overview_density.json. Extraction scripts require the source
  implementation/captures and accept --repo / --captures / --out overrides.
  Routine figure builds read the saved JSON and do not run inference.
